# Hashtags, caption templates, and reply scripts

Everything here is reusable after the 30-day calendar runs out.

## Bio (150 characters max)

```text
Liver health patient advocate 🤍 Living with MASH
Breaking the silence: my story · MASH made simple · plant-based food
Educational, not medical advice ↓
```

Put the website in the link field. Pin three posts: Day 1 (welcome), Day 3 (my story), Day 8 (the stages carousel).

## Caption formulas

Every caption: hook line, one idea, one human detail, one ask. Then hashtags on their own lines.

1. **Education post**
   `[Plain-language claim]. [Why it matters in one sentence]. [What it looks like / list of 3]. [The encouraging part]. [Save or share ask]. [Disclaimer line].`
2. **Story post**
   `[A specific moment, in the first sentence]. [What you felt]. [What you did]. [What you want the reader to take from it]. [Gentle question].`
3. **Recipe post**
   `[One-line why you love it + emoji]. You'll need: [list]. Method: [numbered]. [Why it is liver-friendly, one sentence]. Save ask. "General healthy-eating ideas, not personalized nutrition advice."`
4. **Ask-your-doctor post**
   `"[The question]" [Why this question]. [How to prepare, 3 checks]. Screenshot ask.`
5. **Community post**
   `[A number or a myth]. [One sentence of context]. [Your personal connection]. [Direct question or tag ask].`

## Disclaimer lines (pick one for health posts)

- Educational only, not medical advice. Your care team knows your situation.
- General healthy-eating ideas, not personalized nutrition advice.
- Approximate figures from published liver-health research, shared for awareness, not diagnosis.

## Stories (daily, 15 seconds of effort)

- Monday: poll. "Have you ever had your liver checked?" Yes / No / Not sure.
- Tuesday: photo of the recipe in progress, link sticker to the site.
- Wednesday: reshare the feed post with a one-line "why I posted this."
- Thursday: quiz sticker. "ALT is a… liver enzyme / vitamin / scan."
- Friday: question box. "What do you wish you could ask your doctor?"
- Saturday: your walk, your plate, your tea. Real life, no design.
- Sunday: reshare the best comment of the week (with permission).

## Reel hooks (first 3 seconds, say it to camera)

- "The day I was diagnosed with MASH, I could only think of one person."
- "Your liver can be scarring right now and you would feel completely fine."
- "Three words your doctor might use that mean different things."
- "The blood test you already had might answer a question you never asked."
- "Here is what I eat in a day with stage 3 fibrosis." (end with the disclaimer)
- "If your records say NASH and your new doctor says MASH, here's why."

## Reply scripts

**Newly diagnosed DM**
> I'm so glad you reached out, and I'm sorry you're facing this. I remember exactly how that first week felt. I'm not a doctor, so I can't tell you what your results mean, but I can tell you what helped me: writing down every question before my appointments, asking about a fibrosis assessment, and asking whether a hepatologist and a dietitian should be part of my team. The Living Well page on my site walks through all of it. You're not alone in this. 💚

**"What should I eat?"**
> I eat plant-based because it works for me and my care team is on board, but everyone's needs are different. A registered dietitian can tailor an eating pattern to you. My recipes are on the site if you want ideas to bring to that conversation.

**"Can MASH be reversed?"**
> In earlier stages many people reduce liver fat and inflammation and even improve early scarring with sustained changes and medical care. It depends on the stage and the person, which is why early attention matters so much. Your care team can tell you what's realistic for you.

**Someone sharing a hard story**
> Thank you for trusting me with this. Your experience matters, and sharing it helps someone else feel less alone. Sending you strength. 💚

**Anything that asks for medical advice**
> I'm a patient advocate, not a medical professional, so I can't advise on your specific situation. Please bring this to your doctor. If it helps, here are questions I'd ask: [pick from the Friday list].

## Weekly 10-minute review (Sunday evening)

Open Insights and write down three numbers: reach, saves, profile visits. Note which post of the week had the most saves. Make one more like it next week. That is the whole strategy.
